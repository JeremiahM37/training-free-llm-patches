- **2026-11-03 D008 — Novelty narrowed by reading.** Rank-one weight edits from steering vectors using forward-pass statistics already exist: TFTV (arXiv 2609.09054, NeurIPS 2026; right factor `Σ sign(μ'v_i) σ_i v_i`) and Steer2Edit (2602.09870; right factor `k ∝ W'v`, chosen to covary with the component's own alignment with the steering direction). PSR (2605.03907) has the gated form `ReLU(a·w+b)·z` but trains it by gradient. CAST (2409.05907) separates the condition vector from the behaviour vector but applies it through a thresholded hook. PRISMS (2608.00218) gates tool-call steering with a separate sparse probe, reporting AUC .98-1.00 on Qwen3. **T01 section 1 must not be claimed as new.** The remaining candidate is narrow and possibly just a combination: a right factor fitted in closed form to a labelled *trigger* contrast independent of the steering direction, so "when" and "what" are decoupled inside a plain weight edit. TFTV is added as a required baseline.

Logit-bias per word in E006 is covered by `probe` as the same decision ceiling. Background still contains a small share of trigger-class text (2 of 102 per intent), disclosed rather than fixed.
- **2026-11-03 D010 — H01 form is prior art.** SteerEdit (arXiv 2604.12359) solves `W'K = WK + αZ`, `W'K₀ = WK₀` in closed form with a null-space projection, keyed on trigger-token activations, for stealthy backdoors. That is essentially the LCMV form of T01 with exact nulls rather than minimum energy. It names "task-conditional compilation" as future work. **H01 novelty is now `known` in form.** What may remain: semantic rather than token triggers, K-behaviour composition and capacity, comparison with prompts and routers, label-free compile. The primitive is dual-use: a conditional, weight-resident patch is also what a backdoor is. The write-up must say so, and it must not include any attack evaluation. Patches should ship as separate, signed adapters rather than merged weights.
- **2026-11-03 D011 — Pivot.** Single-behaviour conditional gating is closed on capable models. Next: H19, can it be a plain closed-form LoRA (no hook, no position logic), by regressing key activations to the prompt effect at decision tokens and to zero on background? Also E006 for many behaviours. Fix calibration by regressing magnitudes rather than tuning alpha.

Our claim can only be about the background-zero targets, low rank instead of sparsity, and the task. ICC-style baseline and a background-weight ablation are now required in the final comparison.
